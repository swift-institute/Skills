# Research process

Research reduces a decision’s uncertainty. It is complete when the evidence,
alternatives, and remaining unknowns are explicit—not when enough prose has
accumulated.

## Workflow

1. State the decision, current uncertainty, scope, and stop condition.
2. Separate observed facts, source-backed claims, hypotheses, and preferences.
3. Prefer primary sources and live executable evidence.
4. Inspect the existing ecosystem owner before evaluating new implementation.
5. Compare viable alternatives against the same criteria.
6. Run the operative evaluator for every derived claim; do not substitute a
   proxy that merely resembles it.
7. Record negative results with their search root and known-positive control.
8. Conclude with a recommendation, confidence, risks, and concrete follow-up.

Durable research belongs in the repository that owns the decision. Volatile
state belongs in tools or issues, not in a skill.
