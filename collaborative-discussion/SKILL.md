# Collaborative discussion

Parallel opinions are useful only when they expose independent evidence or
trade-offs. The artifact owner—not a vote—makes the decision.

## Workflow

1. State one decision, its constraints, and the evidence already known.
2. Give each participant a distinct bounded question or critique lens.
3. Require claims to distinguish fact, inference, preference, and unknown.
4. Normalize proposals against the same acceptance criteria.
5. Resolve factual conflicts with source or executable evidence.
6. Identify genuine value trade-offs separately from misunderstandings.
7. Synthesize one recommendation, dissent, confidence, and next validation
   step.
8. Preserve only decision-relevant outcomes; do not retain conversational
   duplication as project memory.
